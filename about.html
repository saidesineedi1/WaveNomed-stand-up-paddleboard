FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about riding at Silver Hoof? We've compiled quick answers for you.

Do I need prior riding experience to take a lesson?

No prior experience is required! We welcome absolute beginners as well as seasoned riders. Our gentle, well-trained lesson horses and certified instructors provide step-by-step guidance tailored to your comfort level.

What should I wear for my first riding session?

We recommend wearing long, comfortable fitted pants (such as breeches or sturdy jeans) and closed-toe boots with a small heel (1 inch) for stirrup safety. ASTM/SEI-certified riding helmets are provided complimentary for all riders.

What are your horse boarding care standards?

Our luxury boarding packages include 12x12 climate-controlled rubber-matted stalls, daily pasture turnout, custom veterinarian-approved grain and hay nutrition, automatic waterers, and 24/7 on-site staff supervision.

Can I book a private trail ride for a special occasion?

Yes! We offer exclusive private sunset trail rides perfect for anniversaries, birthdays, corporate retreats, or family gatherings. Contact our front desk to customize your itinerary.

How do I cancel or reschedule my reservation?

You can easily reschedule or cancel your lesson up to 24 hours in advance free of charge via your Rider Dashboard or by calling our office directly at +1 (800) 555-HOOF.
