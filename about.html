COMMON PADDLER INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about our stand-up paddleboard rentals, clinic curriculums, safety procedures, and dock amenities.

All experience levels are welcome! Over 45% of our guests are first-time paddlers. We offer wide, ultra-stable cruiser boards specifically engineered for zero-wobble balance. Before heading onto the water, every rental includes a complimentary 10-minute dry-dock posture and safety briefing by one of our certified instructors. For those wanting to master technique quickly, our Level 1 Foundations Clinic ensures you paddle smoothly within 30 minutes.

If your goal is relaxing casual paddling, yoga, sunbathing, or paddling with a dog or child, our All-Round Cruisers (33" to 34" width) offer maximum lateral stability. If you want to cover distance, explore distant coves, or glide faster with less effort per stroke, our Carbon Touring SUPs (12'6" to 14' length) feature displacement hull geometry that cuts cleanly through surface chop. Our dock crew will personally match you to the ideal board based on your height, weight, and plans.

Every rental package includes: a high-performance paddleboard, an ultra-lightweight carbon fiber adjustable paddle, a USCG-approved low-profile PFD (life jacket), a coil safety ankle leash, a 15L waterproof drybag for your phone and gear, a safety whistle, and complimentary access to our dockside cedar changing rooms, warm rinse stations, and herbal hydration bar.

Guest safety is paramount. Our water safety director monitors ultrasonic wind and water temperature telemetry 24/7. If gusts exceed 12 knots, lightning approaches within 10 miles, or cold-water advisories are declared, we offer a 100% Risk-Free Weather Refund or Reschedule Guarantee. You will receive an automated SMS advisory at least 90 minutes before your launch time if conditions are deemed unsuitable.

Yes! Children aged 5+ are welcome to tandem-ride on an adult cruiser board with fitted youth PFDs. Youth aged 12+ can pilot their own junior boards under adult supervision. We are also dog-friendly—we have specialized EVA-padded SUPs with claw-resistant deck grips and provide canine life jackets upon request.

We recommend quick-drying athletic wear, boardshorts, or UV-rated rashguards. For dawn or sunset paddles, bring a light windbreaker. Barefoot paddling provides optimal foot traction, though water shoes or neoprene booties work well. Bring sunglasses with a floating retainer strap, reef-safe sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle. Secure dock lockers are provided free of charge for your street clothes.
