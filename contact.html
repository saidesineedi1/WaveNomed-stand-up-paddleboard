Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding visiting, lesson policies, and trail scheduling.

Cancellations or rescheduling requests made at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled session receive a 100% refund or credit. Same-day cancellations may incur a nominal rebooking fee.
