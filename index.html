OUR HERITAGE

The Journey of Silver Hoof

Over two decades of passion, dedicated horse care, and building an equestrian community.

1999

Founding of the Ranch

Eleanor Vance purchased 50 acres of historic farmland to establish a humane sanctuary for rescue horses and traditional riding instruction.

2006

Championship Arena Construction

Inaugurated our 20,000 sq ft covered indoor arena equipped with shock-absorbing dustless footing, hosting regional dressage championships.

2014

150-Acres Trail Network Expansion

Acquired adjacent woodland trails, creating scenic 10-mile private loops featuring creek crossings, wooden bridges, and hilltop vistas.

2020

Equine Wellness & Therapy Center

Launched our dedicated therapeutic riding initiative, assisting veterans and young adults in building emotional resilience through horse partnership.

2025

Luxury Eco-Stables & Today

Unveiled solar-powered climate stabling and private equestrian lounges, setting the gold standard for luxury horse care nationwide.
