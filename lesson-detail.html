Arena Safety & Stables Etiquette

ASTM/SEI Helmets

Certified helmet required at all times when mounted or working in lead rings.

Heeled Footwear

Sturdy boots with a 1-inch heel prevent stirrup slip-through emergencies.

Passing Etiquette

Pass left-shoulder to left-shoulder in ring traffic and yield to faster gaits.

Calm Energy

No sudden movements or loud noises near horses; maintain calm, confident posture.
