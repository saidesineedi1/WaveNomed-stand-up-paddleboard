SAFETY PROTOCOL

Every Paddle Starts With Preparation.

A continuous, five-stage protocol engineered with marine safety standards. From pre-departure analysis to dockside debrief, every step guarantees confidence and situational awareness.

01
PHASE 01 · RADAR & AMBIENT EST. TIME: 5 MIN

CHECK

Evaluate regional weather conditions, thermal winds, alpine water temperature, and lake visibility before launching your craft.

✓ Weather trends & incoming fronts
✓ Wind velocity & thermal vectors
✓ Water conditions & surface swell
✓ Visibility & sunrise / sunset light
02
PHASE 02 · EQUIPMENT RIGGING EST. TIME: 8 MIN

GEAR

Ensure every essential hardware component is inspected, sized correctly to the paddler's height, and securely fastened before entering water.

✓ Paddleboard hull & fin integrity
✓ Paddle height calibrated (+20cm)
✓ Coiled safety leash affixed securely
✓ Personal flotation device (PFD) fitted
03
PHASE 03 · SHORELINE BRIEFING EST. TIME: 5 MIN

PREPARE

Conduct shore-side stability checks, secure personal gear in dry bags, and review the launch corridor and shallow reef hazards.

✓ Secure equipment & dry storage bags
✓ Check board deck traction pad
✓ Check paddle blade orientation
✓ Understand launch area boundaries
04
PHASE 04 · ON-WATER DISCIPLINE SESSION TIME

PADDLE

Remain engaged with your environment, respect right-of-way watercraft lanes, stay within designated zones, and heed guide signals.

✓ Stay within designated navigation areas
✓ Monitor conditions for sudden shifts
✓ Maintain 360° situational awareness
✓ Follow guide & patrol instructions
05
PHASE 05 · RECOVERY & DEBRIEF EST. TIME: 5 MIN

RETURN

Approach the pontoon with speed dampened, safely disembark, rinse equipment, and log session completion with the dockmaster.

✓ Return equipment to dock slips
✓ Report any hardware or seal issues
✓ Confirm session completion on ledger
✓ Enjoy shoreline lounge refreshments
