TRAIL EXCURSION FAQS

Trail Ride Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know before heading out on our countryside trails.

Do I need prior horseback riding experience for trail rides?

No experience is needed! Our Pine Creek Sunset Trek and Rolling Hills Panorama Ride are specially designed for beginners and first-time riders. Our gentle horses walk at a comfortable single-file pace with friendly guide assistance.

What should I wear for a trail excursion?

We require long pants (jeans or riding tights) and sturdy closed-toe shoes or boots with a small heel. We provide ASTM-certified helmets and optional waterproof trail jackets.

Are there age or weight restrictions for trail rides?

Riders must be at least 8 years old for general trail rides (younger children can enjoy pony lessons in the arena!). For horse comfort and welfare, our maximum rider weight limit is 240 lbs.

What is your weather and cancellation policy?

Rides proceed in light rain (we provide trail slickers!). In the event of heavy lightning or storm warnings, you can reschedule free of charge or receive a full refund up to 24 hours prior.
